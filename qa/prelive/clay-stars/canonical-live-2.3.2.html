Source · theory · song

One story, three lenses

The song moves between ancient source material, a modern speculative reading and the human question at their centre. The distinctions below are deliberate.

Ancient source

Clay and flood

Mesopotamian traditions describe divine labour, humanity shaped from clay and blood, and a survivor warned before a devastating flood.

Later theory

Visitors and code

The ancient-astronaut reading recasts gods as travellers, divine blood as genetic code and heavenly fire as technology. It is presented as interpretation, not established history.

The song

What remains ours

The lyric holds both possibilities open, then turns toward agency: whatever our beginning, love, rebellion, memory and the future belong to humanity.

Closing transmission

But the signal remains.”
